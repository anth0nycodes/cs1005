About us

Our story

North Star Bakery started with a simple idea: a neighborhood should have a place that smells like fresh bread at 6 a.m. What began as weekend loaves for friends and neighbors grew into the little shop on the corner you know today.

Every morning, our ovens are warm before the sun is up. We mix, shape, and bake everything by hand in small batches, from crusty sourdough to flaky croissants and cinnamon rolls that are to die for! We’d rather bake a little less and get it right than cut corners to make more. That’s how it’s always been, and how it’s going to stay.

Over the years, we’ve become part of your mornings, your family brunches, and your biggest days. We’ve baked birthday cakes, office breakfast spreads, and wedding dessert tables. Whether you’re grabbing a coffee and a scone on your commute or planning a celebration for a hundred, we’d love to bake for you. Pre-order online or stop by and say hello.

Honoring the Craft

  1. Local Farms

    Our flour, fruit, and eggs come from farms just a short drive away. Buying close to home means fresher ingredients for you and steady support for the growers who make our neighborhood what it is.

  2. Natural Dairy

    We use real butter and whole milk from a regional dairy we’ve worked with for years. No shortcuts, no substitutes. It’s the reason our croissants shatter just right and our cakes stay rich and tender.

  3. Delivering fresh baked goods

    Everything on our shelves is baked the same day. Pre-order online and we’ll have your order ready and waiting at the counter the moment you arrive! Swing by, pick it up, and you’re on your way.
